## 📘 Overview

This repository contains a comprehensive cybersecurity risk assessment for **ABC Retail Store**, a growing retail business with both physical and e-commerce operations. The assessment identifies and evaluates key threats to the business’s digital infrastructure, providing actionable insights to strengthen its overall security posture.

The risk assessment was conducted as part of the Certificate IV in Cyber Security curriculum and demonstrates skills in:

- Threat identification and analysis  
- Stakeholder and asset mapping  
- Qualitative risk rating using industry-aligned frameworks  
- Risk mitigation planning  
- Technical documentation and reporting  

---

## 🏬 Business Background

ABC Retail Store is a dual-channel clothing retailer operating online and in physical locations. As the business scales, maintaining trust and security is a priority—especially in managing sensitive customer and operational data.

This assessment focuses on identifying potential cyber risks that could affect:

- Business operations  
- Legal compliance  
- Customer trust and brand reputation

## 🔐 Critical Information Assets

### Digital Assets
- Customer database  
- E-commerce platform (frontend and backend)  
- Point-of-sale (POS) and inventory systems  
- Email, financial, and HR systems  
- Cloud storage, access control systems

### Physical Assets

## ⚠️ Key Risks Identified

| # | Risk Description | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Rating |
|--|------------------|------------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Unauthorised access to customer data | 4 – Likely | 5 – Catastrophic | **Extreme** |
| 2 | Ransomware attack | 3 – Possible | 4 – Major | **High** |
| 3 | Loss of unsecured mobile device | 4 – Likely | 3 – Moderate | **High** |
| 4 | Insider misuse of admin credentials | 2 – Unlikely | 5 – Catastrophic | **High** |
| 5 | Website downtime due to poor security configurations | 3 – Possible | 4 – Major | **High** |
| 6 | Data loss due to inadequate backups | 2 – Unlikely | 5 – Catastrophic | **High** |

> 🔍 *Each risk includes detailed analysis of actors, assets, vulnerabilities, and business impact. See full documentation for adverse event diagrams and mitigation strategies.*
